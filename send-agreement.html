Send Agreement
Email a client a direct link to sign their engagement agreement. Add a personal note if you'd like, then send. The client signs on mirjcreative.com and is taken straight to their deposit payment.

Every send is logged to the "Invites Sent" sheet inside the Client Agreements folder in Drive. Alignment Reset and Retainer engagements aren't wired to this tool yet, they still use the Long-Term Engagement Agreement PDF.
